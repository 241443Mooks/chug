# Design intent

A reviewer should be able to see what changed, inspect the evidence and decide what to do without reconstructing the comparison themselves.

- Put findings and the next action first. Keep originals, reference wording and full comparison easy to open.
- Show what was checked, what failed and what remains uncertain. Do not use a reassuring status to cover a gap.
- Keep source text, system findings, AI suggestions and human decisions distinct.
- Show earlier decisions with their reasons and limits. Similar wording does not establish the same outcome.
- Remove repeated explanations, unnecessary fields and avoidable clicks.
- Use short, direct wording. Describe what works and what is missing; avoid slogans and claims about quality.

The prototype is intended to test whether this workflow helps. Its appearance and automated tests do not establish that. Use the [human review checklist](CREATIVE_REVIEW.md) to record feedback.
