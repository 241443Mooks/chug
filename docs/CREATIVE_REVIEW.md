# Human review checklist

Use the [local setup](../START_HERE.md#run-locally). Try a routine candidate, a changed document, a flagged exception and an unmatched upload. Then try the separate decision-reuse exercise.

Check whether you can:

- Understand what needs attention without reading technical documentation.
- Reach the exact finding, original wording and reference.
- Tell what was checked and what remains unknown.
- Assess a finding, flag a contract and find the saved record.
- Distinguish earlier context from permission to repeat a decision.
- Use the workflow on a narrow screen and with a keyboard.

Record the task, observed problem, impact and smallest useful change. Choose: accept the experience, request a revision, defer an idea, or stop because of a usability or trust concern.

Automated checks do not substitute for this review. Do not record owner acceptance until the owner has reviewed it. Keep material discoveries in `DESIGN_JOURNAL.md`; retain earlier evidence rather than rewrite history as a success.
